## Price history and chart
- New table `ticker_price_history(ticker, date, close)` (schema change, owner approved), created by `CREATE TABLE IF NOT EXISTS`. `prices.py` now stores every close in the nightly 7-day download (not only the newest) and, for tickers with fewer than 60 stored closes, backfills 3 years (`backfill_history`, chunks of 25). After the first run the backfill costs nothing. `db.price_history` reads it.
- Ticker Price tab: a "Price history" line chart of daily closes (selected currency) with a marker on each day a member traded: triangle up purchase, triangle down sale, diamond exchange, plus the colour (never colour alone), tooltip with member, date, type and amount range. A caption says how many trades are not marked (before the first close or no close within 5 days), and a table of the closes sits in an expander.
- Verified: `scripts/test.sh --smoke` 0 failures; `test_history` (store, overwrite, backfill, failed batch, non-positive closes); real run of `prices.py --limit 30` in a scratch copy (22,560 closes for 30 tickers, 2023-11 to 2026-11); Chromium screenshots at 1100 light and 390 dark for AAPL.
- Expect `congress.db` to grow from about 2 MB to roughly 20 MB once all ~730 tickers are backfilled; the first nightly run after this change is slower (about 30 history batches). Not checked: the full backfill, the deployed app, EUR view, tickers with no history (caption only).

## Health check on the Ticker page
- The Price tab has a "Health check" under the price strip: four cards (How is it priced? Is it profitable and growing? Can it handle trouble? How big and how volatile?), each with a one-sentence factual headline, metrics with tooltips, a range bar (marker = this stock, shaded middle = typical range, thin line = sector median) and a visible line with the zone word, the typical range and the sector median. New metrics: price/sales, price/book, dividend yield, profit margin, return on equity, revenue and earnings growth, debt/equity, current ratio, free cash flow, beta, market cap, 52-week range, analyst consensus and target (worded as opinion). P/E, Fwd P/E and PEG moved into the first card. Zones say lower, typical or higher, never good or bad.

Sector medians come from traded tickers in the same sector (at least 5 values). Logic and the bar HTML live in `fundamentals.py`.
- Verified: `scripts/test.sh --smoke` 0 failures; new `tests/test_fundamentals.py` and `test_fundamentals_update`; Chromium screenshots at 1280 and 390 using a scratch copy of the database filled with invented numbers.
- Real-data check (added later): ran `prices.py --limit 40` locally in a scratch copy with yfinance 1.8 in a throwaway venv: 39 of 40 stocks got metrics (one delisted ticker has none). It found market cap empty for every stock because the positive-number filter capped at 1e6; fixed with `_positive` and a test. Values looked right for AAPL (margin 27.6%, debt/equity 0.84, beta 1.09). Dark card background fixed.
